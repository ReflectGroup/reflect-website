Privacy and terms.

This page sets out how Reflect Group Pty Ltd (ABN 33 647 782 947) handles information provided through this website, and the terms on which the website is made available.

Privacy statement

What is collected

The fee proposal request form asks for a name, company, email address, telephone number, project address, project type, service required, a required-by date, an optional link to drawings and any notes. Only the name and email address are required. No information is collected from visitors who do not submit the form, other than the standard server logs described below.

How it is used

Information submitted through the form is used to prepare and send a fee proposal and to correspond about the project. It is not sold, shared with third parties for their own purposes, or used for marketing beyond the enquiry made.

How it is delivered and stored

Form submissions are delivered by email using Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, which transmits the submission to Reflect Group and may retain a copy for a limited period under its own privacy policy. The submission is then held in Reflect Group's email and project records for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the enquiry and any resulting engagement, and in accordance with Reflect Group's record-keeping obligations.

Drawings and project documents

Drawings and documents provided by link or email are used solely for the purpose of preparing the proposal and, if commissioned, the work. They are treated as confidential and are not disclosed to any other party.

Website hosting and logs

The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare's servers record standard technical information, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and performance purposes under Cloudflare's privacy policy. The website does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not use tracking scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may record the request under Google's privacy policy.

Access and correction

A request to access, correct or delete personal information held by Reflect Group may be made by email to pdjolic@reflectgroup.com.au. Reflect Group is bound by the Australian Privacy Principles to the extent they apply to it.

Terms of use

Information on this website

The content of this website describes the services offered by Reflect Group Pty Ltd and is provided for general information. It does not constitute advice on any particular project, and no reliance should be placed on it in the absence of a written fee proposal and engagement.

Fee proposals and engagement

A request submitted through this website is a request for a fee proposal only. No engagement arises until a written fee proposal has been issued by Reflect Group and accepted in writing by the client. The terms of each engagement are those set out in the fee proposal.
