Approach

How a Reflect bill is prepared.

The order matters. The documentation is fixed first, then measured, then qualified, so that every quantity in the bill can be traced back to a drawing number and revision.

i

Basis of proposal

Every drawing, schedule, specification and report in the set is listed with its revision in an appendix to the proposal. Later revisions or a material change in scope are priced separately.

ii

Measurement in CostX

Trades are measured from the plans, sections and schedules. Reinforcement is taken as detailed or at stated ratios; structural steel in bulk tonnage with a nominal allowance for connections.

iii

Qualification

Where the documents are silent, the assumption is written into the bill. Temporary works, hazardous materials and undetailed joinery are carried as provisional or coverall items so nothing is hidden inside a rate.

Standing conventions
  • No question-and-answer format during preparation. Assumptions are stated in the bill and can be adjusted at tender.
  • Services trades (hydraulics other than stormwater, electrical, mechanical, fire, lift, communications, security) are coverall items unless commissioned in detail.
  • Stormwater drainage and tanks are measured from the hydraulic drawings.
  • Door hardware, joinery and proprietary fitments without schedules are described as coverall items referenced to the detail drawings.
  • Reflect Group does not provide a design service. Obvious discrepancies between drawings and reports are flagged, not resolved.
  • The bill remains the intellectual property of Reflect Group Pty Ltd.
